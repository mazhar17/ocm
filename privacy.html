OBE Course Manager — Privacy

Applies to the application at mazhar17.github.io/ocm and to copies of it served elsewhere. Last updated 5 September 2026.

Where your data lives

OBE Course Manager is a single web page that runs entirely in your browser. There is no server behind it and no account. Everything you enter — courses, outcomes, questions, marks — is stored in your browser's local storage and, if you choose, in a file on your own computer. The application makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting.

Google Drive (optional)

The File menu offers Open from Google Drive and Save to Google Drive as. These are the only features that contact any service, and they do nothing until you click them.

  • What is requested. The Google permission drive.file. It lets the application read and write only the files you pick in Google's own file picker, or files the application itself created. It cannot list, search or read anything else in your Drive.
  • What is sent. Your course data file, to Google's Drive API, into your own Google Drive, when you open or save. Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party.
  • What is kept. The identifier and name of the Drive file you are working on, in your browser's local storage, so the link survives a reload. The sign-in token lives only in memory and expires within an hour.
  • Google's scripts. Two Google scripts (sign-in and the file picker) are loaded from Google when you first click a Drive item — not on page load. Their use is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Revoking access. At any time, at myaccount.google.com/permissions, remove "OBE Course Manager".
